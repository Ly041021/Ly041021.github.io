I am an undergraduate in Robotics Engineering (Honors Program) at Zhejiang University, expecting to graduate in June 2027.

My research at Differential Robotics and FAST Lab, Zhejiang University is advised by Prof. Fei Gao. Previously, I worked on quadrupedal locomotion at MirrorMe Technology, contributing to the White Rhino robot.

I study deep reinforcement learning, embodied perception and state estimation, and motion planning and control, with an emphasis on algorithms that work on real robots under practical sensing and computational constraints.
